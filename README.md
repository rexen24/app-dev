# My Favorite Series: Alice in Borderland

## Why I Was Hooked
I was immediately hooked on *Alice in Borderland* from the very first episode. I love how it combines intense, high-stakes action with **truly clever puzzles**. It's the kind of show that really makes me think, and I kept asking myself what I would do if I were forced to play those deadly games. The whole mystery of who was running the games and why they were in an empty Tokyo kept me on the edge of my seat.

### Here are a few things I think make it amazing:
- The creativity of the games, especially how they correspond to different playing card suits.
- The amazing character development. I really felt connected to the main characters on their journey.
- The constant suspense and the psychological twists, especially in the "Hearts" games.

I definitely recommend it if you like thrillers. [You can check it out on Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/title/80200575).
